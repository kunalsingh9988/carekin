# Account profile extension

Mode: Operate. Extend CareKin's established account/settings surfaces, not its visual identity. The user pins a standard profile structure: signed-in avatar, private profile, photo upload and camera capture.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An identifiable account and a clear separation of private identity from professional care details. No decorative metrics or invented trust claims.

OWN-WORLD: Existing Figtree/Bricolage typography, semantic light/dark paper surfaces, violet selection and actions, established form fields and rounded controls.

STORY: Recognize the signed-in account; preview and explicitly save a portrait; edit private account and role-appropriate care details; find sign-in help and privacy support.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Shared header with avatar, profile heading and a compact violet-tint identity rail. The main column starts with photo actions and personal details. On phones the rail stacks before a full-width editing column. The signature interaction is local photo preview followed by a separate save.

FORM: Direct extension of existing settings and provider-profile forms; code-led, user-specified standard task layout. No concept roll for this precisely scoped extension; seed: none (unavailable).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
